## What Is NOT Yet Achieved

### The cosmology remains speculative

This statement is too strong:

> "Case 1 (E ∝ V) proves that the topological sector modifies the background stress-energy tensor as a true vacuum energy density."

**What it would actually show**:
- $E(V) \propto V$ 
- Therefore $\rho = E/V \approx \text{constant}$
- That is interesting.

**What it does NOT yet prove**:
- Existence of $T_{\mu\nu}^{\text{topo}}$ in required form

**Missing steps**:
1. Definition of the energy being measured
2. Renormalization prescription
3. Pressure derivation
4. Coupling to gravity
5. Consistency with isotropy
6. Consistency with cosmological observations

**Rewritten correctly**:
> "Case 1 would provide *evidence* for vacuum-like scaling and justify *attempting* a stress-energy derivation."

This is scientifically harder to attack because it doesn't overclaim.

## Where the Argument Overreaches

### "Confirms" should be "suggests"

This paragraph:
> "This confirms that $w(z)$ is an emergent trajectory governed by the system settling into its static, index-protected pause depth."

**Current state of the chain**:
```
Forman-Ricci quantities
  ↓
active/pause states
  ↓
w(z)
```

This is still a hypothesis chain. Plausible, but not yet derived.

**Rewrite**: "This *suggests* that w(z) may be emergent..."

The difference is critical: "confirms" implies logical necessity. "Suggests" implies empirical hypothesis.

## The Single Most Important Sentence

Not the dark-energy claim. This one:

> **"Find the conserved quantity first, then determine its energy scaling, then derive (ρ,p), and only then talk about dark energy."**

That is the point where the roadmap starts looking like a disciplined physics program instead of a phenomenological interpretation.

### Why this reorganization matters

1. **Start with $Q$ (topologically conserved)** — mathematics constrains this
2. **Measure $E(V)$ scaling** — numerics determines this
3. **Derive $(ρ, p)$ from mechanics** — theory constrains this
4. **Insert into Einstein equations** — check against observations
5. **Only then claim "dark energy"** — after steps 1–4 pass

This is a defensible research program, not a speculative guess.
