## The Single Most Important Methodological Advance

**Audit discipline**: A result must never move from "measured" to "derived" without a named test.

This prevents:
- Micro evidence blurring into SPARC evidence
- SPARC evidence blurring into cosmological interpretation
- Accumulation of unexamined assumptions

**Implementation**: Every claim in the unified document must carry a status label:
- **Derived**: Mathematically forced from prior assumptions
- **Measured**: Extracted from lattice computation or observation
- **Calibrated**: Fitted to external data
- **Suggestive**: Plausible pattern, not yet verified
- **Open**: Known gap or unresolved question

## Consolidation Principles

### 1. Single Source of Truth
- All claims exist in this one document
- Redundancy eliminated
- Contradictions immediately visible

### 2. Status Labels Are Mandatory
Every factual statement must carry:
- **Derived**, **Measured**, **Calibrated**, **Suggestive**, or **Open**
- Without a label, a statement is incomplete

### 3. Derivational Chains Are Explicit
- "Q → protected sector → vacuum energy" (three-step claim)
- Each step must be verified at transition
- Breaks in chain are flagged as "Open"

### 4. Results Stay Grounded in Methods
- SPARC result: **Measured** (empirical fit to observations)
- Not automatically **Derived** (would need independent theoretical prediction)
- Not automatically **Calibrated** (would need external constraint)

### 5. Falsifiability Is Always Visible
- Volume sweep: **Falsifiable** (clear win/loss criteria)
- Pressure derivation: **Testable** (compare to thermodynamics)
- Cosmological prediction: **Testable** (compare to Planck + DESI)
